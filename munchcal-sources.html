MunchCal — Sources

MunchCal 計算の出典

日本語English

MunchCal の目標カロリーや栄養の目安は、次の研究と公的な基準をもとにしています。

最終更新：2026年11月10日

MunchCal は医療機器ではなく、病気の診断や治療を目的としていません。持病のある方・妊娠中の方・食事制限のある方は、始める前に医師に相談してください。

目標カロリー

1日の目標カロリー

基礎代謝を Mifflin-St Jeor 式で計算し、運動量をかけます。下限は男性 1,500 kcal・それ以外 1,200 kcal。

  1. Mifflin MD, et al. A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals. Am J Clin Nutr. 1990
  2. Frankenfield D, et al. Comparison of predictive equations for resting metabolic rate in healthy nonobese and obese adults: a systematic review. J Am Diet Assoc. 2005

運動量の係数

運動の回数から 1.3〜1.7 をかけます（身体活動レベル）。週0〜2回は 1.3 で、出典の目安 1.4 より少し低めです。

  1. FAO/WHO/UNU. Human energy requirements. 2004

体重を変えるペース

体脂肪 1 kg ≒ 7,200 kcal（脂肪 1 g＝9 kcal、体脂肪の約 8 割が脂肪）として、週のペースを1日あたりに直します。実際の体重の変わり方には個人差があります。

  1. Hall KD. What is the required energy deficit per unit weight loss? Int J Obes. 2008
  2. Hall KD, et al. Quantification of the effect of energy imbalance on bodyweight. Lancet. 2011

栄養の目安

たんぱく質

体重 1 kg あたり 1.6 g（多めを選んだ時は 2.0 g）。筋トレをする大人で、これ以上とっても筋肉は増えなかった量です。

  1. Morton RW, et al. Protein supplementation and resistance training-induced gains in muscle mass and strength. Br J Sports Med. 2018
  2. Jäger R, et al. International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: protein and exercise. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2017

脂質・食物繊維・塩分

脂質はカロリーの 25%（WHO の目安は 30% 以下）。食物繊維は 25 g 以上、塩分は 5 g 未満（WHO）。

  1. WHO. Healthy diet (fact sheet)
  2. WHO. Total fat intake for the prevention of unhealthy weight gain in adults and children: WHO guideline. 2023
  3. WHO. Carbohydrate intake for adults and children: WHO guideline. 2023
  4. WHO. Sodium reduction (fact sheet)

糖分

1日のカロリーの 10% 未満。

  1. WHO. Guideline: sugars intake for adults and children. 2015
  2. WHO. Healthy diet (fact sheet)

水

飲み物から：男性 2.0 L・それ以外 1.6 L。欧州食品安全機関（EFSA）の1日の水の目安（男性 2.5 L・女性 2.0 L）から、食べ物から入る約2割を引いた量です。

  1. EFSA. Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for water. EFSA Journal. 2010
  2. National Academies. Dietary Reference Intakes for Water, Potassium, Sodium, Chloride, and Sulfate. 2005

体重と BMI

BMI

体重(kg) ÷ 身長(m)²。区分は WHO の基準です。

  1. WHO. Obesity and overweight (fact sheet)
  2. NIH NHLBI. Calculate your BMI

歩数と運動

歩いて使ったカロリー

歩数 × 体重 × 0.0005 kcal のおおよその値（ふつうの歩き 約 3 METs・1,000 歩 ≒ 10 分）。

  1. Ainsworth BE, et al. 2011 Compendium of Physical Activities. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 2011
  2. Compendium of Physical Activities

歩数の目安 10,000 歩

1日の歩数が多いほど死亡リスクが下がり、6,000〜10,000 歩ほどで横ばいになるという研究です。

  1. Paluch AE, et al. Daily steps and all-cause mortality: a meta-analysis of 15 international cohorts. Lancet Public Health. 2022
  2. WHO. Guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour. 2020

MunchCal’s calorie goal and nutrition targets are based on the research and public guidelines below.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

MunchCal is not a medical device and is not meant to diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or follow a special diet, talk to a doctor before you start.

Calorie goal

Daily calorie target

Resting energy from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, multiplied by your activity level. Never below 1,500 kcal for men and 1,200 kcal for others.

  1. Mifflin MD, et al. A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals. Am J Clin Nutr. 1990
  2. Frankenfield D, et al. Comparison of predictive equations for resting metabolic rate in healthy nonobese and obese adults: a systematic review. J Am Diet Assoc. 2005

Activity factor

1.3–1.7 based on how often you work out (physical activity level). For 0–2 workouts a week we use 1.3, slightly below the source’s 1.4.

  1. FAO/WHO/UNU. Human energy requirements. 2004

Weekly pace

Uses about 7,200 kcal per 1 kg of body fat (9 kcal per gram of fat; body fat is about 80% fat) to turn your weekly pace into a daily amount. Real weight change varies from person to person.

  1. Hall KD. What is the required energy deficit per unit weight loss? Int J Obes. 2008
  2. Hall KD, et al. Quantification of the effect of energy imbalance on bodyweight. Lancet. 2011

Nutrition targets

Protein

1.6 g per kg of body weight (2.0 g if you chose more protein). In adults doing strength training, eating more than this did not add more muscle.

  1. Morton RW, et al. Protein supplementation and resistance training-induced gains in muscle mass and strength. Br J Sports Med. 2018
  2. Jäger R, et al. International Society of Sports Nutrition Position Stand: protein and exercise. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. 2017

Fat, fiber and salt

Fat is 25% of calories (WHO: 30% or less). Fiber is at least 25 g and salt less than 5 g a day (WHO).

  1. WHO. Healthy diet (fact sheet)
  2. WHO. Total fat intake for the prevention of unhealthy weight gain in adults and children: WHO guideline. 2023
  3. WHO. Carbohydrate intake for adults and children: WHO guideline. 2023
  4. WHO. Sodium reduction (fact sheet)

Sugar

Less than 10% of daily calories.

  1. WHO. Guideline: sugars intake for adults and children. 2015
  2. WHO. Healthy diet (fact sheet)

Water

From drinks: 2.0 L for men and 1.6 L for others. This is EFSA’s daily water intake (2.5 L for men, 2.0 L for women) minus about 20% that comes from food.

  1. EFSA. Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for water. EFSA Journal. 2010
  2. National Academies. Dietary Reference Intakes for Water, Potassium, Sodium, Chloride, and Sulfate. 2005

Weight and BMI

BMI

Weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Categories follow the WHO.

  1. WHO. Obesity and overweight (fact sheet)
  2. NIH NHLBI. Calculate your BMI

Steps and activity

Calories from walking

Roughly steps × weight × 0.0005 kcal (normal walking about 3 METs; 1,000 steps ≈ 10 minutes).

  1. Ainsworth BE, et al. 2011 Compendium of Physical Activities. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 2011
  2. Compendium of Physical Activities

10,000-step goal

More daily steps are linked to lower mortality, leveling off around 6,000–10,000 steps.

  1. Paluch AE, et al. Daily steps and all-cause mortality: a meta-analysis of 15 international cohorts. Lancet Public Health. 2022
  2. WHO. Guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour. 2020

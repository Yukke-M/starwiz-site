Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This policy applies to consumers in Washington State and Nevada and describes how STARWIZ INC. handles "consumer health data" in its apps Pakumi, Sofafit and Cozpi. Our general Privacy Policy is separate.

1. Categories of consumer health data we collect and why

  • Body and nutrition (Pakumi): sex, age, height, weight, target weight, goals, meal records and estimated nutrition, meal photos and descriptions, water intake records. Purpose: to track meals and weight and to estimate nutrition with AI at your request.
  • Steps (Pakumi on iPhone, optional): today's step count, read from Apple Health only if you allow it. Purpose: to show your steps and an estimate of calories burned walking. Step data is used only on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party, and Pakumi never writes to Apple Health.
  • Fitness and safety (Sofafit): answers to safety questions (heart disease or high blood pressure, chest pain, dizziness or fainting, chronic conditions and medication, bone/joint/muscle problems, doctor-supervised exercise), workout records, perceived effort, stopping because of pain, sit-to-stand counts. Purpose: to suggest a safe workout version and track your workouts.
  • Wellbeing (Cozpi): sleep, waking, activity level, stress, support, routine, self-care focus areas, journal entries and moods, breathing-tool history. Purpose: to suggest habits and self-care tools and track them.
  • Usage events (all apps): limited values derived from the above (e.g. whether a workout was stopped because of pain, which lighter or seated version was chosen, Cozpi setup answers, only if you agree to send them), linked to a random per-device number. Purpose: to improve the apps.

Most of this data is stored only on your device. It leaves your device only for AI analysis (Pakumi), optional backup (Sofafit, Cozpi) and usage events, as described in our Privacy Policy. When the Pakumi app has no food illustration for a dish estimated by AI, it also sends the English dish name to our server so we can add more food art; we store only a count per dish name, without photos or anything that identifies you.

2. Sources

You (what you enter or photograph in the apps) and your device (app usage).

3. Categories of consumer health data we share

  • Meal photos and meal descriptions (Pakumi, only after you give permission in the app)
  • Backup copies of your app records (Sofafit, Cozpi, only if you sign in with Apple)
  • Usage events described in Section 1

We do not sell consumer health data.

4. Third parties and affiliates we share with

We share consumer health data only with service providers (processors) that act on our behalf: cloud hosting and database (Supabase, Inc.), generative AI analysis (Google LLC, Gemini API; Anthropic, PBC, Claude API as a fallback). We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.

5. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data and access it (including a list of the third parties with whom it was shared), withdraw your consent, and request deletion. Email miyashita@starwiz.co.jp with the subject "Consumer health data request" and the app name. You can withdraw consent to AI analysis in Pakumi Settings > "AI analysis" and stop backup by signing out. We will respond within 45 days (extendable by 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice). If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision by email; if the appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov) or the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov).

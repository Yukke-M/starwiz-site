Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This policy explains how STARWIZ INC. ("STARWIZ," "we," "us") collects, uses and shares "consumer health data" in its apps MunchCal, Sofafit and CozySide (the "Apps"). It is written for residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act), Nevada (SB 370), Connecticut and other U.S. states with similar laws, and it supplements our general Privacy Policy.

1. Key points

  • Most of your records stay on your device. They leave your device only for the purposes described below.
  • We do not sell consumer health data, and we never use it for advertising or marketing.
  • We share consumer health data only with service providers that process it on our behalf.
  • We do not use geofencing around health care facilities.

3. Sources

We collect consumer health data from you (what you enter, photograph or record in the Apps), from your device (App usage) and, for MunchCal step counts on iPhone, from Apple Health with your permission.

4. How we use consumer health data

  • To provide the features you request, as described in Section 2
  • To improve the Apps and fix bugs, using usage events
  • To keep the Services secure and comply with the law

We will ask for your consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data for any other purpose.

5. Consumer health data we share and with whom

  • Categories shared: meal photos and descriptions (MunchCal, only after you consent); backups of your App records (only if you sign in); usage events described in Section 2.
  • Recipients: only service providers (processors) that act on our behalf under contract: Supabase, Inc. (servers, database and sign-in; data stored in Seoul, Republic of Korea) and Google LLC (Gemini API, for MunchCal photo and text analysis). Under Google's terms for paid Gemini API use, Google does not use this data to improve its products.
  • Affiliates: we have no affiliates that receive consumer health data.
  • We may also disclose consumer health data when required by law.
  • We do not sell consumer health data.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access that data, including a list of all third parties and affiliates with whom we have shared it and their contact information
  • Withdraw your consent to our collection and sharing of your consumer health data
  • Have your consumer health data deleted
  • Appeal our decision on your request

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. How to exercise your rights

  • In the Apps: delete your data in MunchCal under Settings > Account > Delete account (signed-in users), in Sofafit under "About your data" at the bottom of Settings > Delete account and data, and in CozySide under Settings > Your data > Delete account and data. Withdraw consent to AI analysis in MunchCal Settings > "AI analysis," revoke Apple Health access in the Health app, and turn off usage events in Sofafit and CozySide Settings ("Share usage data"). Stop backups by signing out.
  • By email: email miyashita@starwiz.co.jp with the subject "Consumer health data request," the App name and how you signed in (Apple, Google or email), if at all. We will verify your identity before acting on your request.
  • Timing: we will respond within 45 days of receiving your request. If reasonably necessary, we may extend this by another 45 days and will tell you why.
  • Appeals: if we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision by email. We will respond to your appeal within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov/file-complaint), the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov) or the Connecticut Attorney General (portal.ct.gov/ag), as applicable.

8. Security and breach notification

We protect consumer health data with measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and database access controls, as described in Section 12 of our Privacy Policy. If a breach of your consumer health data occurs, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by law, including the Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule where it applies.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. If we change how we collect or share consumer health data, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent. Questions: STARWIZ INC., 2-2-15 Minami-Aoyama, Minato-ku, Tokyo 107-0062, Japan, miyashita@starwiz.co.jp.
